# ChargePoint

- **HQ:** United States
- **Regions:** North America, Europe
- **Site:** https://www.chargepoint.com/drivers/home

Home Flex: 16–50 A @ 208/240 V (max 12 kW), J1772 or NACS, NEMA 6-50/14-50 or hardwire. Plug-in limited to 32/40 A. UL 2594/2231, ENERGY STAR. Wi-Fi, ChargePoint app. Commercial AC stations (CT4000 etc.) are a different product line.
